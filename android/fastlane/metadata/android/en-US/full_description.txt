Ghost is the Android client for the Qompass AI Ghost protocol: anonymous,
Tor-only email on your own Ghost onion service.

All traffic stays inside the Tor network. The app connects to your Ghost
server's .onion address for IMAP and SMTP, so your IP address is never
exposed to the mail server or to recipients' servers.

Features in v1:
- Tor bootstrap with progress, kept alive by a foreground service
- IMAP inbox and SMTP send over Tor's SOCKS proxy
- OpenPGP message encryption (Bouncy Castle)
- Credentials in encrypted on-device storage; backups disabled

No Google Play Services, no Firebase, no tracking. Fully open source.
